ESP32-S3 · phyphox · 2-DOF pan-tilt

Pan-Tilt Motion Lab

A smartphone running phyphox streams Roll, Pitch and Yaw to an ESP32-S3, which turns them into Pan and Tilt servo angles. This page tracks project progress and replays recorded motion through every data pre-processing step, one window per step.
