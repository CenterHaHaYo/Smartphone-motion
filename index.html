Pan-Tilt Motion Lab

Read Roll, Pitch and Yaw from a smartphone (phyphox), clean the signal with a low-pass, moving-average or Kalman filter, then map it to Pan and Tilt to drive the servos of a pan-tilt FPV camera mount on an ESP32-S3.

    Motion

    The phone is held sideways like a camera: Yaw turns it left/right → Pan, Pitch nods it up/down → Tilt, Roll tips it sideways (not used).

    – Replay

    Blue rays = where each camera points. Dashed ray = target before the speed limit. Drag a view to turn it, double-click to reset.

    Raw Roll, Pitch, Yaw from phyphox

    Degrees, exactly as the phone sends them. Yaw drives Pan, Pitch drives Tilt, Roll is not used.

    Drag across any chart to zoom · double-click to reset · hover to see the same moment in every chart.

    3

    Sample timing

    Time between two readings. The ESP32-S3 asks the phone every 100 ms (10 Hz).

    4

    Filter

    Three ways to smooth the signal, each with the values set in its code. Pick one to drive the servos.

    5

    Dead-band

    The output only moves when the filtered angle changes by more than the dead-band, so small shakes never reach the servos.

    6

    Map to Pan / Tilt

    Angle range → servo range, clamped to the limits, then moved at most the max speed every 20 ms.

    Yaw → Pan servo
    Pitch → Tilt servo
    7

    Frequency content (FFT)

    Which shake frequencies are in the signal, and how much the chosen filter removes.
